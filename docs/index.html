03 / BEHIND THE NUMBERS

Useful heat makes a fair comparison.

01. Start with demand

All systems deliver the same annual useful heat. Oil uses an editable energy content in kWh per litre and boiler efficiency. Electricity uses your all-in unit rate.

02. Account for performance

Heat pump electricity = heat demand ÷ seasonal COP. Gas and pellets use efficiency-adjusted fuel costs. Annual maintenance is included; gas also includes an incremental standing charge.

03. Treat solar as a contribution

Solar thermal offsets a share of hot-water demand, capped at total heat demand. Backup oil, solar pump electricity and both systems’ maintenance remain included.
